# Four Seasons — Product Page Description

**Title:** Four Seasons: An Adult Coloring Collection
**Author:** MBOQ Colors

## Description

Step into a year of color with **Four Seasons**, a collection of 40 original
stained-glass-inspired coloring pages celebrating spring, summer, autumn,
and winter.

Each design is built from the small, familiar details of the changing
year — flowers and vines unfurling in spring, waves and sunbursts of summer,
acorns, pinecones, and drifting leaves in autumn, and snowflakes, frost, and
icicles in the quiet of winter. Bold black outlines and richly patterned,
stained-glass-style linework turn every page into an intricate mosaic of
interlocking shapes, flowing bands, and layered motifs — never a repeat, and
never a page that feels like a template.

Every design is abstract and ornamental rather than realistic, so there's no
"right" way to color it and no pressure to get it perfect. That makes this
book a calm, meditative escape as much as a creative one — pick up a pencil
or marker and lose yourself in a pattern for a few minutes or a few hours.

**What's inside:**
- 40 original, single-sided designs — nothing lost to bleed-through
- Printed on one side of each 8.5" x 11" page for clean removal and display
- High-contrast black-and-white line art, ready for markers, gel pens, or colored pencils
- A dedicated "This book belongs to" page — makes a thoughtful gift
- No two designs alike — every page is a fresh pattern to discover

Whether you're unwinding after a long day or looking for a relaxing gift for
a fellow colorist, **Four Seasons** offers a full year of intricate,
anxiety-free coloring, one season at a time.
